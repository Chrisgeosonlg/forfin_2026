A confluence shaping secure finance

Two days where finance, regulation and AI-driven security meet.

FORFIN 2026 — Fortifying Finance — convenes the leaders accountable for trust across Tanzania's financial and government sectors. Building on a landmark first edition, this year turns to the defining challenge of the decade: keeping institutions resilient as AI reshapes both attack and defence.

Expect candid keynotes, hands-on workshops, working panels and the kind of executive networking that turns into partnerships — all in an immersive retreat setting on the Indian Ocean.

Attendance is by invitation only.

The venue

White Sands Resort & Conference Centre.

FORFIN 2026 is hosted at the renowned White Sands Resort & Conference Centre on Jangwani Beach — a prime setting that pairs modern conference facilities with the serene charm of the Indian Ocean.

Spacious meeting rooms with ocean views and a calm, secure environment provide the perfect backdrop for focused dialogue, knowledge-sharing and high-level networking.
