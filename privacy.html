Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FORFIN and Computer Centre Tanzania handle personal information connected with this website and the FORFIN 2026 event.

Who we are

FORFIN is an event organised by Computer Centre Tanzania. For questions about this policy or your personal information, contact us at forfin@cctz.co.tz.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when contacting us, including your name, email address, enquiry type and message. If you attend or participate in the event, we may also collect information needed to manage invitations, registration, attendance and event communications.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and communicate about FORFIN.
  • To administer invitations, attendance, partnerships and event participation.
  • To maintain the security and proper operation of our website and event.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

We use personal information only where we have a legitimate purpose, your consent, or another lawful basis.

Cookies and browser storage

This website uses local browser storage to remember the privacy choice made in the consent banner. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. Embedded or linked services, such as Google Maps, Google Fonts, YouTube and social media websites, may receive technical information or use their own cookies when you load or interact with them. Their privacy policies govern that processing.

Sharing and international transfers

We may share information with trusted service providers and event partners only where necessary to operate the website or deliver the event. Some third-party services may process information outside Tanzania. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are used and do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above or to meet legal obligations. We apply reasonable organisational and technical measures to protect it, although no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Some requests may be limited by legal obligations. To make a request, email forfin@cctz.co.tz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

Effective date: 31 August 2026
